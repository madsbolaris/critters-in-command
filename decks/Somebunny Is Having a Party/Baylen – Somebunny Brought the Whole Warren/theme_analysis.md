# Theme Analysis — Somebunny Brought the Whole Warren

> Baylen meant to mark the Second Harvest in the Season of the Burrow with a quaint little party beneath The Party Tree — and then *somebunny* invited the whole warren. Every card is scored below for how well it fits **"Somebunny Brought the Whole Warren,"** the Naya rabbit-tribal token-combo deck where every guest brings a plus-one, every plus-one brings a twin, and the guest list swells from a headcount into a regional census — right up until Craterhoof stomps the evening to a close.

### Scoring Guide
- **10**: Perfect — rabbit + party + core synergy
- **7–9**: Strong — clearly on-theme (rabbit or party flavor + good synergy)
- **4–6**: Moderate — functional connection to the party
- **1–3**: Weak — pure power card, minimal flavor connection

## Theme Summary

A ~100-card Naya rabbit-tribal token-combo deck.

- **Rabbits (by creature type):** 9 of 91 unique cards — plus a swarm of rabbit *tokens*.
- **Rabbit art:** 17 cards, almost all Bloomburrow originals.
- **Party connection:** 60 of 91 cards tell the "Somebunny Brought the Whole Warren" story through name, flavor, or function.
- **Average theme score:** 4.8

### Key Takeaways

- The tribal core is tight and focused — a small set of true Rabbits that snowball into a board-flooding token swarm.
- Highest theme density lives in the Creatures and Sorceries; the mana base is the lowest, as expected.
- Pure-power, low-flavor inclusions (Sol Ring, Chrome Mox, Sylvan Library, Enlightened Tutor, Seedborn Muse) are here to win games, not because they were invited to the party.
