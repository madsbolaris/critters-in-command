# Baylen, the Haymaker — Somebunny Brought the Whole Warren

[Baylen, the Haymaker](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/345/baylen-the-haymaker) meant to mark the [Second Harvest](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/123/second-harvest) in the [Season of the Burrow](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/29/season-of-the-burrow) with a quaint party beneath [The Party Tree](https://scryfall.com/card/ltc/348). But somebunny had other ideas.

By afternoon, [Harvestrite Host](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/15/harvestrite-host) has transformed the clearing into a [Utopia Sprawl](https://scryfall.com/card/woc/135/utopia-sprawl) of lanterns, garlands, and long tables. Baylen’s [Caretaker’s Talent](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/6/caretakers-talent) promises to keep every guest fed, entertained, and thoroughly charmed. A [Tocasia’s Welcome](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/181/tocasias-welcome) awaits everyone at the entrance, and [Harvestrite Host](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/15/harvestrite-host) installs a [Halo Fountain](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/285/halo-fountain) at the center of it all, a gleaming promise that the night will end in triumph.

And then the guests arrive. [Hare Apparent](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/15/hare-apparent) brings nine suspiciously similar plus-ones, [Warren Warleader](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/38/warren-warleader) charges in with more rabbits at her heels, and [Head of the Homestead](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/216/head-of-the-homestead) appears with an entire entourage. [Valley Questcaller](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/36/valley-questcaller) welcomes every newcomer like the guest of honor, [Rabbit Battery](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/157/rabbit-battery) sends [Jacked Rabbit](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/9/jacked-rabbit) charging straight onto the dance floor, and [Regal Bunnicorn](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/25/regal-bunnicorn) stands quietly in the corner becoming alarmingly large. [Enduring Vitality](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/176/enduring-vitality) and [Seedborn Muse](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/268/seedborn-muse) keep everybunny dancing long after sensible rabbits have gone to bed, while [Clever Concealment](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/ONC-5/clever-concealment) keeps the party out of sight.

But word spreads. An [Eladamri’s Call](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/197/eladamris-call) joins the [Chord of Calling](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/134/chord-of-calling) rolling across the hills, carrying news that the party of the season is already underway. Someone shouts [Assemble the Legion](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/163), every neighboring warren takes it personally, and one cry of [Hop to It](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/16/hop-to-it) returns as a [March of the Multitudes](https://scryfall.com/card/ncc/346/march-of-the-multitudes). [Anointed Procession](https://scryfall.com/card/akh/2/anointed-procession), [Doubling Season](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/216/doubling-season), [Mondrak, Glory Dominus](https://scryfall.com/card/one/23/mondrak-glory-dominus), and [Ojer Taq, Deepest Foundation](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/26/ojer-taq-deepest-foundation) ensure that every arrival brings several more rabbits no one remembers inviting. The guest list becomes less of a list and more of a regional census.

For one shining moment, Baylen looks across the clearing and decides this must be [Faith’s Reward](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Faith%27s+Reward%22): every neighboring warren gathered beneath the lanterns, dancing together as though the night might never end. Every newcomer insists on paying their share with a [Smothering Tithe](https://scryfall.com/card/p22/5/smothering-tithe), burying the gift tables beneath pies, flowers, blankets, and carrots. [Impact Tremors](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/717) shake the rafters with every arrival, and the celebration swells toward a [Grand Crescendo](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/118/grand-crescendo).

By midnight, however, the buffet is empty, the tables are buckling, and no one can remember who was invited. Good manners give way to [Survival of the Fittest](https://scryfall.com/card/exo/129/survival-of-the-fittest), while the search for the final carrot cake slice becomes a [Cathars’ Crusade](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/17/cathars-crusade) sweeping from table to table. Rabbits climb the garlands, defend abandoned plates, and claim entire sections of the dance floor as sovereign territory. The bells, drums, and thousands of dancing paws tighten into a [Skullclamp](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/283/skullclamp) around Baylen’s temples.

The chaos begins to move as one and the celebration becomes a [Beastmaster Ascension](https://scryfall.com/card/ncc/283/beastmaster-ascension) as thousands of paws fall into a perfect [Pawpatch Formation](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/186/pawpatch-formation), and the shouting becomes a roar. The roar becomes a charge.

And then the last invitation is answered. The [Finale of Devastation](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/289/finale-of-devastation) brings [Craterhoof Behemoth](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/404/craterhoof-behemoth) crashing through the tree line. One stomp sets the whole warren in motion, and nothing between the dance floor and the horizon survives.

**Everybunny hopped in. Everybunny trampled out.**
