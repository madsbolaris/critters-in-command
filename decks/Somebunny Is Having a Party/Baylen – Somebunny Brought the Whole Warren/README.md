| **Good against** | Slower decks without early interaction, control decks light on sweepers, and decks that can't race a turn 4-6 clock |

## Somebunny Brought the Whole Warren

**[Baylen, the Haymaker](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/345/baylen-the-haymaker)** meant to mark the [Second Harvest](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/123/second-harvest) in the [Season of the Burrow](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/29/season-of-the-burrow) with a quaint party beneath **[The Party Tree](https://scryfall.com/card/ltc/348)**. But somebunny had other ideas.

By afternoon, *[Harvestrite Host](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/15/harvestrite-host)* had transformed the clearing into a [Utopia Sprawl](https://scryfall.com/card/woc/135/utopia-sprawl) of lanterns, garlands, and long tables, and had installed a **[Halo Fountain](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/285/halo-fountain)** at the center of it all, a gleaming promise that the night would end in triumph. *[Jetmir, Nexus of Revels](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/193/jetmir-nexus-of-revels)* took one look, decided that isn’t nearly enough, and struck a [Dawn’s Truce](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/9/dawns-truce) with the coming morning so the party could run as long as it likes. As guests begin to trickle in, the host’s [Caretaker’s Talent](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/6/caretakers-talent) promises to keep every guest fed, entertained, and charmed, starting with a [Generous Gift](https://scryfall.com/card/lcc/128/generous-gift) for whoever arrives first.

But the trickle doesn’t last. [Hare Apparent](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/15/hare-apparent) brings eleven suspiciously similar plus-ones, [Warren Warleader](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/38/warren-warleader) charges in with more rabbits at her heels, and [Head of the Homestead](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/216/head-of-the-homestead) appears with an entire entourage, while one cry of [Hop to It](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/16/hop-to-it) answers with a litter of its own. [Valley Questcaller](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/36/valley-questcaller) welcomes every newcomer like the guest of honor, [Jacked Rabbit](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/9/jacked-rabbit) takes over the dance floor, and [Regal Bunnicorn](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/25/regal-bunnicorn) stands quietly in the corner becoming alarmingly large.

[Enduring Vitality](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/176/enduring-vitality), [Seedborn Muse](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/268/seedborn-muse), and a well-timed [Vitalize](https://scryfall.com/card/wth/145/vitalize) keep everybunny dancing long after sensible rabbits have gone to bed, dancing hard enough to [Raise the Past](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/22/raise-the-past) and drag the early sleepers back onto the floor.

[Clever Concealment](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/ONC-5/clever-concealment) attempts to keep the party out of sight, but word spreads. An [Eladamri’s Call](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/197/eladamris-call) joins a [Chord of Calling](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/134/chord-of-calling), rallying others to [Assemble the Legion](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/163) for the biggest party of the season. A [Panharmonicon](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/310/panharmonicon) and [Thrumming Stone](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/315/thrumming-stone) carry it even farther, past the treeline and into warrens that never got an invitation.

The reply comes back as a [March of the Multitudes](https://scryfall.com/card/ncc/346/march-of-the-multitudes); every neighboring warren takes it personally. [Farmer Cotton](https://scryfall.com/card/ltc/55/farmer-cotton) rolls in from the next warren over with a wheelbarrow full of [Delighted Halflings](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/158/delighted-halfling), and a [Springleaf Parade](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/19/springleaf-parade) follows right behind them. [Anointed Procession](https://scryfall.com/card/akh/2/anointed-procession), [Doubling Season](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/216/doubling-season), and [Parallel Lives](https://scryfall.com/card/isd/199/parallel-lives) ensure an [Abundant Growth](https://scryfall.com/card/ecc/97/abundant-growth) of rabbits no one remembers inviting. The guest list becomes less of a list and more of a regional census. 

[Impact Tremors](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/717) shake the rafters with every arrival, and the celebration swells toward a _[Grand Crescendo](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/118/grand-crescendo)_ as thousands of paws fall into a perfect [Pawpatch Formation](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/186/pawpatch-formation). Soon they're loud enough to [Awaken the Woods](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/170/awaken-the-woods). But the dance floor was never built for this many guests, and it strains and buckles beneath them; a crew rushes to [Secure the Wastes](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/203), but even they can’t keep up with the wreckage.

And at the [Hour of Reckoning](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/120/hour-of-reckoning), just when it seems the party can’t possibly hold any more, one last invitation goes out and **[Craterhoof Behemoth](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/404/craterhoof-behemoth)** answers. One stomp sets the whole warren in motion, and in a [Finale of Devastation](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/289/finale-of-devastation), nothing between the dance floor and the horizon survives.

Everybunny hopped in. Everybunny trampled out.

**RSVP: regretfully, everyone.**
