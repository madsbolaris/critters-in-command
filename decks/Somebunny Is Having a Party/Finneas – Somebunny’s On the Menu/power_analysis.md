# Power Analysis — Finneas, Ace Archer · "The Potluck" (B4)

> Card-by-card evaluation of **raw power and function** (independent of flavor), plus a cohesion verdict. Companion to [theme_analysis.md](theme_analysis.md).

### Power Scale
A rough 1–10 read of how much each card *does* in this shell (not its constructed pedigree): 8–10 = engine/bomb, 6–7 = strong role-player, 4–5 = filler/glue, ≤3 = flavor tax.

### The pitch
A **Food-fueled counters/lifegain engine** on a wide **Rabbit** board. A Food token is 3 stored life on demand, and every payoff converts life into advantage: Heliod (life → +1/+1 counters), Archangel of Thune (life → board pump), Well of Lost Dreams (life → cards). Finneas showers the whole crowd — every token *and* every Rabbit — with a counter each attack; the Ozolith package banks those counters through removal. It grinds on permanent counters and a resilient lifelink board rather than a one-turn overrun, and keeps a compact combo (Heliod + Spike Feeder → infinite life → Felidar Sovereign) as an inevitability button.

### How it differs from Baylen
Baylen is an explosive Hare-tribal stampede that dumps a board and swings once with Coat of Arms + Craterhoof. The Potluck keeps the token doublers (they double **Food** here) but leaves out Coat of Arms, Craterhoof, and Finale — it wins by grind, permanent counters, and lifegain inevitability.

## Combo note
**Heliod, Sun-Crowned + Spike Feeder** makes infinite life, which becomes a win with **Felidar Sovereign** (40-life upkeep) or infinite +1/+1 counters with **Archangel of Thune**. All pieces are on-theme lifegain/counter cards that earn their slots anyway, giving the deck an inevitability button without a dedicated combo package. Appropriate for Bracket 4.

## Verdict
Focused and redundant: a Food battery feeds a lifegain-into-counters machine, three doublers multiply every token type and Food, and Finneas plus Cathars'/Master Chef bury a wide Rabbit board in permanent counters. It grinds and stabilizes (lifelink + Food + protection) where Baylen races, and keeps a soft combo for closing. Weaknesses: a few of the small Rabbits are role-players rather than bombs, and an un-answered wipe on an empty hand still stings — mitigated by The Ozolith, Resourceful Defense, six protection/insurance cards, and the sheer volume of Food to rebuild on.
